Strategic decisions

The calls that shaped the engine.

How I set up the team and the system at Transworld GCC, and why.

01

One SDR per funnel

One SDR on outbound acquisition with ICP campaigns, one on inbound: WhatsApp, website, valuation requests and dormant leads. Each person owns one funnel and one number.

02

Outreach the company owns

Mailboxes under the company and a dedicated sending domain. The engine stays when people change roles, and the main domain’s reputation stays protected.

03

Content judged on seller demand

The content function is measured on the owners it brings in, not posts published, so the team writes for pipeline.

04

Hire for the biggest upside

When I moved up, I redesigned my old role as SEO & Growth with partnerships, instead of hiring a like-for-like replacement.

05

WhatsApp as the main door

GCC owners answer WhatsApp faster than forms, so it runs site-wide with a one-hour reply standard in working hours.
