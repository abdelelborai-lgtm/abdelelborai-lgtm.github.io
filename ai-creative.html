Workflow Direction first, AI second

A production process, not a lucky prompt.

01

Brief

What the product is, what it claims, where it runs, what the image has to do.

02

Research

The category, competitors and the brand's own rules.

03

Direction

Mood, light, surface and shot list, agreed before anything is generated.

04

Prompts

Built to reuse across the product line so the shots match.

05

Generation

Image and video passes tied back to the real product photo.

06

Photoshop

Compositing, retouching, label fixes, colour and cleanup by hand.

07

Final assets

Cut to every size: feed, story, reel, Snap and TikTok.
