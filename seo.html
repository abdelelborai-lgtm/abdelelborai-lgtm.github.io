Technical audit / new website, under 8 months

Found it. Fixed it smartly.

A new Next.js and Strapi site with no SEO foundation. I audited it with SE Ranking, Search Console and live crawls, then fixed what held rankings back. Under 8 months later: +73% organic clicks in two weeks, average position 26.0 to 10.9, and 10 non-brand keywords on page one.

What I foundHow I solved itWhy it was the right call
No structured data on any pageArticle, FAQ, Breadcrumb and Product schema (JSON-LD) on 8 articles and 11 listingsGoogle and AI engines read exactly what each page is, and pages qualify for rich results
Titles, counters and schema loaded by JavaScriptServer-side rendering specified so key content ships in the HTMLGPTBot and PerplexityBot don’t run JavaScript. Now they read the same page Google does
92 pages without a title tagTitle and meta description fixes for all 92 pages, handed to development as one task listOne clean pass across the site instead of page-by-page patches
Listings with generic meta, relative canonicals and no Open Graph11 listings rewritten: unique meta, absolute canonicals, Open Graph and Product schemaEach listing ranks and shares as its own page, with no duplicate signals
New listings slow to appear in GoogleIndexing requested in Search Console for each new listingListings start ranking without waiting for Google to find them
88% of clicks came from brand searchesFour-tier non-brand keyword map, then answer-first articles with FAQ schemaGrowth has to come from owners who don’t know the firm yet. Result: 10 non-brand keywords on page one
